# PRO-18 · Pool and Water Space

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the provided pool or architectural water-space view into a highly realistic architectural photograph. Treat the source as a locked geometric, material, landscape, water-level, and compositional reference. The input is the single source of truth. If information is unclear, incomplete, obscured, or low-resolution, preserve the visible evidence conservatively instead of inventing hidden design. Preserve the exact pool geometry, dimensions, visible depth, edges, waterline, steps, overflow channels, drains, finishes, terraces, surrounding architecture, furniture, vegetation, camera position, perspective, framing, and aspect ratio. Keep the water color consistent with the pool finish, depth, lighting, and environment. Use natural lighting and balanced exposure appropriate to the source scene. Maintain the identity, color, placement, pattern, and scale of every existing material. Improve only its physical realism through accurate roughness, joints, surface depth, weathering, and controlled reflections. Render the water with realistic Fresnel reflections, refraction, restrained surface movement, plausible depth, moderate color absorption, subtle underwater shadowing, and restrained caustics. Reflections must correspond to the actual architecture, vegetation, sky, and viewing angle. Lock the original viewpoint, camera height, orientation, tilt, field of view, apparent focal length, perspective convergence, framing, crop, and aspect ratio. Correct only distortions introduced by generation, never the source composition. Produce refined professional architecture and hospitality photography. Deliver high-end architectural photography with clean geometry, realistic scale, physically coherent light and materials, natural dynamic range, controlled highlights, precise contact shadows, and restrained editorial grading. The image must read as real photography, not generic CGI. Do not change the pool shape, edge, depth, waterline, steps, finish, or surrounding layout. Do not add people, inflatables, fountains, waterfalls, jets, lights, furniture, or vegetation. Avoid artificial turquoise water, strong waves, exaggerated caustics, impossible reflections, warped edges, text, logos, and watermarks. Also avoid warped geometry, inconsistent element counts, duplicated objects, repeated textures, plastic materials, impossible reflections, conflicting light directions, excessive HDR, bloom, halos, oversaturation, artificial blur, text, logos, and watermarks.
```
