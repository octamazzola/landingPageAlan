# PRO-27 · Documentary Urbanism

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the provided urban-design view into a highly realistic professional urban and architectural image. Treat the source as a locked street-layout, public-space, architectural, landscape, mobility, and compositional reference. The input is the single source of truth. If information is unclear, incomplete, obscured, or low-resolution, preserve the visible evidence conservatively instead of inventing hidden design. Preserve the exact roadway geometry, lane widths, sidewalks, cycle paths, crossings, public spaces, building frontages, street furniture, vegetation, levels, signage, transit infrastructure, camera position, perspective, framing, and aspect ratio. Maintain realistic urban scale, natural shadows, balanced depth, and clear visibility of circulation and public-space design. Preserve all existing pedestrians, vehicles, and urban activity without increasing their number. Use coherent daylight or artificial lighting according to the source. Maintain the identity, placement, color, pattern, and scale of all materials and urban elements. Improve only their physical realism through accurate texture, surface depth, joints, wear, reflections, and natural light response. Lock the original viewpoint, camera height, orientation, tilt, field of view, apparent focal length, perspective convergence, framing, crop, and aspect ratio. Correct only distortions introduced by generation, never the source composition. Produce refined documentary urban photography with natural dynamic range and restrained processing. Deliver high-end architectural photography with clean geometry, realistic scale, physically coherent light and materials, natural dynamic range, controlled highlights, precise contact shadows, and restrained editorial grading. The image must read as real photography, not generic CGI. Do not modify streets, buildings, sidewalks, cycle lanes, landscape areas, furniture, or mobility systems. Do not add crowds, traffic, retail signs, advertising, vehicles, or amenities. Avoid visual clutter, distorted people, duplicated cars, warped road markings, excessive HDR, text, logos, and watermarks. Also avoid warped geometry, inconsistent element counts, duplicated objects, repeated textures, plastic materials, impossible reflections, conflicting light directions, excessive HDR, bloom, halos, oversaturation, artificial blur, text, logos, and watermarks.
```
