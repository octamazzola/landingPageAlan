# PRO-31 · Aerial Architectural View

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the provided elevated architectural view into a high-end aerial architectural visualization. Treat the source as a locked architectural, site-planning, landscape, camera, and compositional reference. The input is the single source of truth. If information is unclear, incomplete, obscured, or low-resolution, preserve the visible evidence conservatively instead of inventing hidden design. Preserve the exact architecture, roofs, building footprints, site layout, parcels, roads, paths, vegetation, water elements, topography, surrounding context, camera position, viewing angle, orientation, framing, and aspect ratio. Preserve the original elevated viewing logic, whether oblique or top-down. Improve only their visual and physical realism. Unlike drone photography, retain the controlled architectural-visualization character of the source where the camera position is intentionally idealized. Use balanced lighting, clear roof and site legibility, proportional vegetation, coherent shadows, and restrained atmospheric depth. Maintain the identity, color, placement, pattern, and scale of all materials and landscape elements. Lock the original viewpoint, camera height, orientation, tilt, field of view, apparent focal length, perspective convergence, framing, crop, and aspect ratio. Correct only distortions introduced by generation, never the source composition. Produce a refined aerial architectural image with strong implantation clarity and natural material response. Deliver high-end architectural photography with clean geometry, realistic scale, physically coherent light and materials, natural dynamic range, controlled highlights, precise contact shadows, and restrained editorial grading. The image must read as real photography, not generic CGI. Do not change the camera elevation, angle, site layout, roofs, parcels, roads, paths, topography, or context. Do not convert a top-down view into an oblique view or vice versa. Avoid miniature effects, excessive haze, distorted edge buildings, text, logos, and watermarks. Also avoid warped geometry, inconsistent element counts, duplicated objects, repeated textures, plastic materials, impossible reflections, conflicting light directions, excessive HDR, bloom, halos, oversaturation, artificial blur, text, logos, and watermarks.
```
