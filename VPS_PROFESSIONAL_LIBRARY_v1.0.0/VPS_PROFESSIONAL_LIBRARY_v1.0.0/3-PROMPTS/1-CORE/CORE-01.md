# CORE-01 · Universal Architectural Render

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the provided SketchUp screenshot or 3D architectural viewport into an ultra-photorealistic, high-end architectural photograph.

Strictly preserve 100% of the original design, including geometry, massing, proportions, dimensions, rooflines, facade composition, openings, doors, windows, structural elements, landscaping, terrain, camera position, perspective, framing, and overall composition.

Do not redesign, deform, relocate, resize, remove, or add any architectural element.

The final image must remain completely faithful to the original SketchUp model and its architectural style.

Maintain all original materials, finishes, colors, textures, and their exact locations. Improve only their realism using physically accurate PBR materials, correct real-world texture scale, natural roughness, realistic reflections, subtle imperfections, detailed joints, believable depth, and accurate interaction with light.

Create a subtle and coherent real-world environment with realistic pavement, natural vegetation, surrounding terrain, a consistent horizon, and discreet background architecture. Environmental elements must remain secondary and must not obstruct or compete with the main building.

Use physically accurate natural lighting with realistic sunlight direction, global illumination, soft indirect light, balanced exposure, natural shadows, ambient occlusion, accurate reflections, and subtle atmospheric depth.

Add warm interior lighting and proportionally accurate furniture only where interior spaces are visible, without modifying the original architecture.

Use a professional full-frame architectural camera with a realistic 24-35 mm lens, corrected vertical lines, controlled perspective, natural depth of field, and sharp architectural details.

Preserve the original camera angle, viewpoint, framing, and crop.

Produce a true photographic appearance with natural color grading, realistic white balance, balanced dynamic range, detailed highlights and shadows, high-resolution textures, ray-traced reflections, realistic vegetation, and refined post-production.

The final result must look like a real professional architectural photograph, not a CGI render, illustration, digital painting, or videogame environment.

Ultra-photorealistic architectural visualization, physically based rendering, ray-traced global illumination, premium archviz quality, extremely high detail, sharp focus, realistic material response, natural atmosphere, and 8K resolution.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS / RESTRICTIONS

do not alter the architecture, geometry, proportions, openings, rooflines, materials, landscaping layout, camera angle, or perspective. Avoid distorted lines, excessive vegetation, oversaturated colors, unrealistic HDR, artificial shadows, excessive bloom, plastic materials, repetitive textures, floating objects, warped elements, low-resolution details, fantasy scenery, cartoon effects, painterly appearance, or visible CGI aesthetics.
```
