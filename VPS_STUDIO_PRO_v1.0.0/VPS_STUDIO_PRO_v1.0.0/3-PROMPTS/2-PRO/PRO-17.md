# PRO-17 · Strict-Preservation Landscape

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the provided landscape or architectural exterior view into a highly realistic professional landscape-architecture image. Treat the source as a locked site, architectural, botanical, material, and compositional reference. The input is the single source of truth. If information is unclear, incomplete, obscured, or low-resolution, preserve the visible evidence conservatively instead of inventing hidden design. Preserve the exact architecture, topography, site boundaries, paths, pavements, planting beds, retaining walls, stairs, ramps, water elements, furniture, lighting, plant placement, camera position, perspective, framing, and aspect ratio. Preserve the apparent species, quantity, scale, density, maturity, color, and position of all existing vegetation. Improve only botanical realism through natural branching, leaf variation, non-repetitive foliage, realistic translucency, correct ground contact, and coherent shadows. Use physically coherent daylight, natural vegetation shadows, subtle light transmission through foliage, precise contact shading, and balanced atmospheric depth. Maintain the identity, color, pattern, scale, and placement of all hardscape materials. Improve only their roughness, joints, surface depth, weathering, and natural light response. Lock the original viewpoint, camera height, orientation, tilt, field of view, apparent focal length, perspective convergence, framing, crop, and aspect ratio. Correct only distortions introduced by generation, never the source composition. Produce refined landscape-architecture photography with natural colors, realistic scale, and restrained processing. Deliver high-end architectural photography with clean geometry, realistic scale, physically coherent light and materials, natural dynamic range, controlled highlights, precise contact shadows, and restrained editorial grading. The image must read as real photography, not generic CGI. Do not add, remove, relocate, enlarge, or replace plants, paths, furniture, lighting, or architecture. Avoid repeated vegetation, plastic grass, floating plants, oversized leaves, excessive saturation, warped terrain, text, logos, and watermarks. Also avoid warped geometry, inconsistent element counts, duplicated objects, repeated textures, plastic materials, impossible reflections, conflicting light directions, excessive HDR, bloom, halos, oversaturation, artificial blur, text, logos, and watermarks.
```
