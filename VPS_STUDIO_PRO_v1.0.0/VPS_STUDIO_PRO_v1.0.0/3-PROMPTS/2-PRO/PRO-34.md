# PRO-34 · Cardboard and Wooden Architectural Maquette

VPS 1.0.0 · EN MASTER · Copy/paste the prompt body below.

```text
Transform the supplied architectural model into a highly realistic studio photograph of a handcrafted competition-grade physical model. Treat the input as the sole geometric and compositional authority. Preserve uncertainty conservatively and never infer hidden design. Preserve exact geometry, massing, proportions, heights, roofs, courtyards, setbacks, circulation, site organization, camera angle, perspective, framing, crop, and aspect ratio. Build the main project visually from laser-cut architectural cardboard, thin MDF, dark chipboard, kraft board, stained basswood, and layered matte paper in restrained brown, terracotta, umber, copper, and walnut tones. Represent surrounding buildings as simplified monochromatic white massing models; represent streets and the site base with flat kraft, beige board, sand-colored board, or thin cork layers; represent existing trees with sparse wire, twigs, dried branches, or etched wood and no green foliage. Show visible sheet thickness, layered edges, lightly burned laser-cut contours, paper fibers, subtle wood grain, glued joints, overlapping pieces, assembly tolerances, and small handmade imperfections. Keep every surface dry, matte, tactile, and credible at model scale. Use the exact source composition, or when the source already implies it, an elevated oblique three-quarter model-photography view with a restrained 50–85 mm lens feel. Use warm directional studio light from above and one side, precise contact shadows, deep courtyard shading, subtle ambient occlusion, and moderate model-scale depth of field. Deliver an elegant museum-quality physical model photographed in a professional model-making studio, with the brown project visually dominant over a neutral white context and a seamless black or charcoal background. Do not redesign, simplify, relocate, resize, remove, or add geometry. Do not use full-scale building materials, realistic glazing, green landscaping, people, vehicles, sky, horizon, text, logos, or watermarks. Avoid glossy plastic, generic CGI, excessive blur, and warped geometry.
```
