Do not widen the driveway or invent a curb cut, gate opening or paved extension. Preserve every visible driveway edge. If the existing hardstand cannot contain the full vehicle with usable clearance, do not insert it.

ARCHITECTURE LOCK  Preserve every existing architectural and landscape element, geometry, proportion, opening, joint, material, texture, curb, paving mark, road marking, sign, furniture item, plant, person, existing vehicle, sky, viewpoint, crop, aspect ratio, camera position, focal behavior, light direction and color identity. No blocked pedestrian route, accessible path, entrance, fire access or traffic lane. No readable license plate, brand logo, advertising, duplicate vehicle, warped wheel, floating tire, melted bodywork, inconsistent reflection, false transparency, halo, edge contamination, excessive motion blur or visible AI artifact. Do not place the vehicle on lawn, steps, a pedestrian-only path or in front of a closed gate with no usable depth.

FINAL OUTPUT  Deliver one professional architectural visualization in which the vehicle is secondary to the project, physically credible and optically inseparable from the original image, while the visible architecture remains unchanged.
